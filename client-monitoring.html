How It Works

Verification doesn't end at intake.

A client's information is accurate the day it's collected. After that, things change. Addresses go stale, phone numbers are disconnected, new medical treatment creates records that matter to the case.

CG360 monitors client data on an ongoing basis and alerts firms when something material changes. This means firms stay in contact with their clients and have current information when it matters, without assigning someone to manually check.

Contact Information Changes
Detect changes to client contact information so the firm can keep records current and maintain communication throughout the case.
Health Record Updates
As clients continue treatment, new medical records become available. CG360 can retrieve additional medical records and track any changes so the firm's case file stays current.
Material Change Alerts
When something changes that the firm needs to know about, CG360 generates an alert rather than requiring someone to check manually.
Need to get current on existing cases?
CG360 can check for changes to client information and retrieve updated medical records across your active caseload — not just cases going forward.
