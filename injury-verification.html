How It Works

Records and assessment, in one step.

For many firms, getting health records is the starting point for evaluating a case. But retrieving records is only half the problem. Understanding what those records say about the strength of the claim is where the real value is.

CG360 handles both. We select the right retrieval method based on the case type and client situation, optimizing for cost and speed when possible. Once records are in hand, we assess the injury claim based on what they show, giving firms a clear picture of injury severity and whether the evidence is consistent with the reported incident.
