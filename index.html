Why It Matters

Accurate client data
is the foundation.

Every decision a firm makes about a case depends on the quality of the client data behind it. When identity isn't confirmed, contact information goes stale, or injury claims aren't assessed, the downstream consequences are real: wasted investment, lost clients, and cases that never reach resolution.

CG360 integrates with multiple data sources to deliver verification, assessment, and monitoring through a single platform, so firms can focus on practicing law instead of managing data vendors.
