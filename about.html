What We Do

Three services for case-level clarity.

Firms make better decisions when they have accurate client data, a clear picture of the injury claim, and current information throughout the life of the case. CG360 provides all three through a single platform, helping firms reduce wasted spend and focus their resources on the cases that matter.

Client Verification

Confirm client identity and validate key information at the point of intake, before investing in the case.
Injury Verification

Retrieve health records and assess injury claims so case evaluation is informed by evidence, not assumptions.
Client Monitoring

Track changes to client information through the life of the case without manual follow-up.
Already have cases in progress?
The same three services can be applied to your existing caseload. It's a fast way to surface gaps in client data, reassess injury claims, and get current on cases that may have gone stale.
